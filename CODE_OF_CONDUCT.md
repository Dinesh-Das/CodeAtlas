# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in CodeAtlas a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Focus technical disagreement on evidence and tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help make the project welcoming to newcomers.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and sustained
personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the contact method in
the repository's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the
extent practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.
